Release timeline

One capability step at a time, each gated by the benchmark suite.

  1. v0.1.0Oct 10, 2026

    2D CPS3/CPS4 linear static, Abaqus-style input, JSON CLI, VTU output, and browser UI.

  2. v0.2.0Oct 10, 2026

    C3D8/C3D4 3D elements, direct sparse fallback, Gmsh import, and MCP server mode.

  3. v0.3.0Oct 10, 2026

    Modal and buckling analyses, B31/CPS8/CPE8 breadth, multi-step loads, and mode viewing.

  4. v0.4.0Oct 10, 2026

    Steady-state heat, sequential thermal stress, and Newmark transient dynamics.

  5. v0.5.0Oct 10, 2026

    Updated-Lagrangian geometry, J2 plasticity, and 2D node-to-surface contact.

  6. v0.6.0Oct 10, 2026

    Solvix output-database frames, history plots and animations, PNG export, and threaded assembly/PCG.
